SEO Plan for Nexus Dev Studio
Site reviewed: nexus-devstudio.vercel.app
The site is built with Next.js (visible from the _next URLs), which is good for SEO
because pages can be rendered on the server. This document lists what I found and the
steps to take, in order.
Problems found
1. The site is one page. Services, Work, Reviews and Contact are all sections of the
homepage (/#services). Google can only rank pages, so right now there is one page
competing for everything. This is the biggest issue.
2. The domain doesn't match. The site runs on nexus-devstudio.vercel.app, but the
tags point to nexusdevstudio.com. Google gets mixed signals, and a vercel.app
address looks less trustworthy to clients.
3. The meta description is too long. Google cuts it off at about 155 characters, and the
current one is around 210.
4. The keywords are vague. "AI engineering & full-stack studio" is not something people
type into Google. They search things like "web development company in Lahore" or
"Flutter app developer".
Step-by-step plan
Step 1: Connect your real domain
In Vercel, go to Settings → Domains and add nexusdevstudio.com, if you own it. Then
redirect the vercel.app address to it.
Step 2: Set up Google Search Console
Add your domain, verify it, and submit your sitemap. This tells Google your site exists.
Step 3: Create separate pages

Step 4: Fix the tags on every page
Title: under 60 characters, with the keyword first. Example: Flutter App Development
Company | Nexus Dev Studio
Description: under 155 characters, written so people want to click
Canonical URL: add a unique one to each page
Step 5: Add a sitemap and robots.txt
In Next.js, add app/sitemap.ts and app/robots.ts. Then open /sitemap.xml and
/robots.txt on the live site to check they work. These could not be verified during the
review.
Step 6: Add FAQ schema
Since the FAQs are ready, wrap them in FAQ structured data (JSON-LD). Also add
Organization and Service schema. Test it for free with Google's Rich Results Test.
Step 7: Build trust and links
This is where most beginners stall.
Create a Google Business Profile
Keep the same name, email and phone number on Fiverr, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook
and LinkedIn, and link them all to the website
Add an email address or contact form next to the WhatsApp button, because some
visitors won't use WhatsApp
